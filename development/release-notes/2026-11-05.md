# 2026-11-05

## Kirman's Herding

In this update nomos's agents herd ([decision 0005](../decisions/0005-kirmans-herding.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0074](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0074-perception-and-decision-in-the-economy-atlas.md)).

- **A market that herds without a preference.** Kirman's model joins Schelling's: agents hold one of two views, change it alone now and then, or are recruited by those of the other view. Below a threshold the market spends most of its time near one view or the other and switches suddenly, though no agent prefers either. The model runs on every schedule of `agents` and carries the exact law of the number holding a view, against which a long run is checked.
- **Its first use.** The Economy Atlas draws a market of 40 agents and its law beside the exact one.
