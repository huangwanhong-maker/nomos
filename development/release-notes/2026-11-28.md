# Release Notes: 28 November 2026

## 1. Progress Toward Declared Targets

- **The trend to target** ([decision 0019](../decisions/0019-progress-toward-declared-targets.md)). `measurement` now measures how far a series has come toward a declared target, after the United Nations Statistics Division's method: the share of the way covered, the progress expected by the target's year, and the class of progress that results. The communities' book on the Sustainable Development Goals read every indicator through it.
