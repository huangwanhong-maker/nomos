# Release Notes: 20 November 2026

## Allocation, and Principles as Orders over Allocations

nomos can now say, when there is not enough, who is served first, what each receives and how much, by rules whose properties are known; and it can compare allocations under several principles of distribution without adding them into one score ([decision 0015](../decisions/0015-allocation-and-welfare-orders.md)).

- **How much.** More rationing rules for a sum short of its claims: the TAL family between equal losses and equal awards (the Talmud's rule at its middle), Piniles's rule, adjusted proportional, any rule's dual, weighted proportional and priority classes. The formulas public bodies use: sufficiency first (shortfalls to a threshold closed before anything else, rationed when they cannot all be), Porto Alegre's banded points, and fiscal equalisation of needs against capacities, each reporting its parts apart.
- **Who first.** Reserve systems give each category its own order of priority and are processed in a declared precedence; their three axioms are checked, and who is served under every order of the categories is reported, since the order is itself a choice. Serial dictatorship, the random serial dictatorship and probabilistic serial assign indivisible goods, and a random assignment is carried out as a lottery over deterministic ones.
- **Whether a division is fair.** Envy-freeness, envy-freeness up to one good, EFX, proportionality and the maximin share are tested, and round-robin, envy-cycle elimination and the maximum Nash welfare divide goods with their known guarantees.
- **What to fund by votes.** Utilitarian greedy, the Method of Equal Shares and sequential Phragmén, with extended justified representation checked: a majority of six and a minority of four show greedy giving the four nothing and equal shares giving them their due.
- **Principles as orders.** Total, prioritarian, leximin, sufficiency (shortfalls below a threshold) and equality of opportunity are each an order over allocations; Lorenz, generalised Lorenz and sequential dominance hold whatever the parameter. Options are compared under all principles at once: the undominated, the principles favouring each of a pair, and where a parameter such as aversion to inequality flips a comparison. A weighted aggregate appears only where weights are declared.
- **Checks:** 37 and 13, held to the Talmud's estates, Pathak and his colleagues' hard reserve left idle, and the axioms on random instances.
