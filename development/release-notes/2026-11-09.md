# 2026-11-09

## Accessibility

nomos can now say how much of what people need lies within their reach, and who is left without it ([decision 0008](../decisions/0008-accessibility.md)). Given where people live, where services are and how long each journey takes, it counts what lies within reach or weighs it by nearness. It also shares each service among everyone who can reach it (the two-step floating catchment), so that a crowded clinic counts for less. From that it finds the people with nothing within reach and those far below the average, measures how unequal access is, and gives the World Bank's Rural Access Index. The measures take journey times from any source: a street network, a road map, or a table of costs.

## Panels, Instruments and Differences in Differences Move Out

In this update nomos's econometrics keeps what is particular to the social sciences: the marginal effects of logit and probit, the multinomial logit, and Poisson regression with absorbed effects for the gravity of trade ([decision 0007](../decisions/0007-panels-instruments-and-differences-move.md)). Panels went to tyche, and instruments and differences in differences to peira, with their checks, so that every science that studies repeated units or assignments it did not control reaches them in the library of designs.
