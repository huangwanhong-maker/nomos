# 2026-11-07

## Composite Indicators

In this update nomos combines factors on scales of their own into one index ([decision 0006](../decisions/0006-composite-indicators.md)): each scaled between two percentiles, so that an outlier leaves the rest in place, inverted if more of it is worse, and averaged by weights, a factor missing for a unit left out of its mean. The Urban Science Workbench lets one weigh such an index of a region and see the columns follow, and says what the index is: a description by those weights, not a measure.
