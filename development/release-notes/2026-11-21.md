# Release Notes: 21 November 2026

## Behaviours That Say Where They Came From

An agent's behaviour now declares its origin, a written rule, a learned policy or a language model's proposal, with what it may observe and do ([decision 0016](../decisions/0016-behaviours-of-three-origins.md)). A behaviour is shown only what it declared and is refused an act it did not declare; a run counts the decisions each origin took, so that a result says how much of it model-generated behaviour decided, and stands as computed, estimated or proposed accordingly. A learned linear policy can be fitted from a record and carries its fit with it.
