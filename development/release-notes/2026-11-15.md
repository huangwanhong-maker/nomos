# 2026-11-15

## Microsimulation of Taxes and Benefits, and Psychometrics

A declared set of taxes, contributions and benefits can now be applied to every household of a weighted sample ([decision 0013](../decisions/0013-microsimulation-and-psychometrics.md)), and a reform compared with its baseline in several ways:

- in revenue and inequality;
- in poverty against a fixed line;
- by decile, with its gainers and losers;
- in the marginal effective and participation tax rates that the rules combine to.

A scale's reliability can be measured by Cronbach's α and its kin. Items can be fitted by Rasch's and the two-parameter model by marginal maximum likelihood, and persons placed on the trait.
