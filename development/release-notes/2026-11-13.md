# 2026-11-13

## Appraisal, Futures, and the Agreement of Coders

nomos can now compare alternatives on several criteria, weigh a project's costs and benefits over time, structure a panel's judgement of the future, and say how far coders agree ([decision 0012](../decisions/0012-appraisal-and-agreement.md)). This serves the infrastructure's programme of the material and ecological conditions of life, where options for a city or a household are compared, and where field notes are coded.

- **Alternatives compared** (`appraisal`):
  - a screen that sets aside, whatever its other merits, an alternative that breaks a limit;
  - the weighted sum, TOPSIS, PROMETHEE II, and AHP's weights with its consistency ratio by Saaty's random indices;
  - how far a weight can move before the first alternative changes;
  - with weights only partly known, how often each alternative ranks first (SMAA).
- **Costs and benefits.** Net present value and the internal rate of return, discount rates that decline over time, switching values, discounted payback, and weights for who gains, so that a sum to a poorer group can count for more.
- **Futures.** The rounds of a Delphi panel until it agrees and its median holds, and morphological fields with the configurations that pass the consistency of every pair.
- **The agreement of coders** (`measurement`): Cohen's κ for two coders, plain or weighted linearly or quadratically; Fleiss's κ for many; and Krippendorff's α for any number with missing codes, at the nominal, ordinal, interval and ratio levels.

It is held to 34 checks: by hand, against closed forms (a cycle of judgements has λ = 1 + 9 + 1/9), and against numpy, statsmodels and the krippendorff package as cross-checks.
