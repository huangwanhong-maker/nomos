# 2026-11-04

## Agent-Based Models

In this update nomos runs agent-based models ([decision 0004](../decisions/0004-agent-based-models.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0071](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0071-agents-and-decisions-for-urban-planning.md)).

- **Schedules stated, never hidden.**
  - Synchronous ticks, run on a pool of threads and the same on any number of them.
  - Random sequential ticks.
  - Gillespie's exact simulation in continuous time, on a tree of rates.
- **Reproducible by identity.** Each agent's draws at each tick come from a stream of their own, and a run's events are logged, so that a domain can analyse a simulated history as an observed one.
- **Spaces and choices.** Lattices, graphs and the plane; logit choice; Schelling's model of segregation, with its description in the ODD protocol.
- **Held to exact laws.**
  - A small model's law against the master equation.
  - A large one against its mean field.
  - The voter model's chance of consensus against its initial share.
  - Schelling's sorting: from half like neighbours to three quarters at a threshold of a third.

## nomos Founded: Inequality and Indices, Games, Matching, Choice, and Econometrics

nomos begins as the foundation for the methods of the social sciences ([decision 0002](../decisions/0002-scope-boundaries-and-dependencies.md), [decision 0003](../decisions/0003-the-first-modules.md)), standing on tyche for regression and on calx for linear algebra and the simplex method. One can now:

- measure how unequal a distribution is (the weighted Gini coefficient, the Lorenz curve, Theil's indices and the generalised entropy family split within and between groups, Atkinson's indices, the Palma ratio), how poor (the Foster–Greer–Thorbecke family and the Watts index, also by group), how concentrated a market is (Herfindahl–Hirschman, concentration ratios, numbers equivalents), and how specialised (location quotients, which are Balassa's index, and the Grubel–Lloyd index of two-way flows);
- compare prices across time with the index numbers of the CPI Manual (Laspeyres, Paasche, Fisher, Törnqvist, Walsh and others, the elementary indices, direct or chained), held to the Manual's own artificial data;
- solve two-player games: pure and mixed equilibria by support enumeration, iterated elimination of dominated strategies by pure strategies or by mixtures, zero-sum games by linear programming;
- match two sides of a market by deferred acceptance from either side, with capacities, checked for stability, and trade houses by top trading cycles;
- count ballots by plurality, Borda, approval and instant runoff, and by the Condorcet methods (Copeland, Schulze, Kemeny–Young);
- estimate panel models (within, one-way or two-way on unbalanced panels, between, random effects and Hausman's test), instrumental variables (two-stage least squares, the first stage's F, Sargan and Hansen's tests), differences in differences, average marginal effects of logit and probit, and Poisson pseudo-maximum likelihood with exporter and importer effects absorbed, the estimator of gravity equations, without forming its hundreds of dummies.

Random effects carries the GLS covariance on Swamy and Arora's scale, so that Hausman's statistic cannot come out negative when the effects are correlated with the regressors. 242 checks, against closed forms, brute force, published examples and the dummy-variable regressions the absorbed estimators must equal.
